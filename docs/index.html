Start here

The homepage at index.html is the original 2020 HUD: five ECharts IIFEs, jQuery, and flexible.js. Arrays used to live only inside js/index.js. They now also live under data/ so examples can reload them without copy-paste.

Two root files, draw1.js and draw2.js, were option objects that nothing mounted. The gallery pages plantar pressure and tissue thickness are the first places those series become charts.

Regenerate

After editing a dashboard array:

python3 scripts/extract_data.py && python3 scripts/validate_data.py

That rewrites JSON, examples/js/datasets.js, and generated-stats.md.

Limits

  • Personal visualization archive, not a clinical dataset.
  • No sampling protocol, city, device, or subject IDs shipped.
  • Bilateral pies are a log formula, not prevalence.
  • Shoe last on the scatter is centimetres, not Mondopoint.
