原首页把五张图挤在一屏。下面按“先看什么、别误读什么”写下书桌批注，方便把仪表盘当图版而不是当海报。

The homepage packs five charts onto one screen. These notes say what to look at first and what not to over-read, so the dashboard can be used as a plate rather than a poster.

.bar

1. 身高与鞋码：先找斜率，再找离群点

1. Height and shoe: slope first, then outliers

两性几乎平行（斜率约 0.14 cm 鞋长 / cm 身高）。框选工具能把某一段身高单独放大。最小值标记会跳到最矮、最短的点，不一定是“异常儿童”。散点没有年龄颜色，所以不能从这一张推出生长速度。

The two sexes are nearly parallel (about 0.14 cm of shoe length per cm of height). Brush a height band to zoom. Min markers jump to the shortest pair, which is not automatically an “outlier child.” There is no age color, so this panel cannot show growth speed.

.line

2. 年龄与脚长：看交叉，而不是看柱顶装饰

2. Age and length: watch the crossover, not the diamond caps

6–8 岁女童均值略高，11 岁起男童领先，14 岁差约 0.5 cm。菱形是 pictorialBar 装饰。8→9 岁两组都略回落，年鉴把它记作源码里的拐点，而不是要解释的生理事件。

Girl means sit slightly higher from 6–8; boys lead from 11; the gap is about 0.5 cm at 14. The diamonds are pictorialBar caps. Both series dip from 8 to 9 — the yearbook records that as a source kink, not a physiological event to explain.

.bar1

3. BMI 与胖瘦度：两条线，不是因果

3. BMI and shape: two lines, not a cause

数值随 BMI 上升，但不能把它读成“BMI 决定脚型”。右侧环图是另一套占位比例，分子分母都不在折线序列里。读这张图时把环图当成装饰比例，把折线当成主序列。

Values rise with BMI; that is not “BMI causes foot shape.” The rings are a second, placeholder ratio whose numerator and denominator are not in the line series. Read the lines as the main series and the rings as decoration.

.line1

4. 双脚比例：先关掉自动播放

4. Symmetry: pause autoplay first

时间轴默认 2 秒一帧。要比较某一岁，先点暂停。越往后“相同”越大，是公式衰减，不是随访队列。五档百分比加总为 100%。

The timeline advances every two seconds. Pause it before comparing a single age. The climb in “same feet” is the decay formula, not a followed cohort. The five bins add to 100%.

5. The center is a radar; the map art is decoration
