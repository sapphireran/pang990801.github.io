这份副本承认的事实

What this copy treats as fact

  • 女童 / 男童散点各 199 点，合并后身高与鞋长相关约 r = 0.92。
  • 6 到 14 岁，女童脚长约增 4.11 cm，男童约增 4.89 cm。
  • 女童脚长在 11 岁附近被男童超过（差值由正转负）。
  • 双脚“相同”一档从 2 岁约 63% 升到 14 岁约 91%，但这是源码里的对数衰减公式，不是逐人实测。
  • 雷达每根轴满刻度是 100，是相对分，不是厘米或千克。
  • 199 girl points and 199 boy points; pooled height vs shoe length is about r = 0.92.
  • From age 6 to 14, mean foot length rises about 4.11 cm for girls and 4.89 cm for boys.
  • Girl mean length is overtaken by boys around age 11 (the signed gap turns negative).
  • “Same feet” rises from about 63% at age 2 to 91% at age 14 — that series is a log-decay formula in the source, not person-level measurements.
  • Radar spokes are capped at 100. They are relative scores, not cm or kg.

这份副本不声称的事

What this copy does not claim

不是诊疗建议，不是抽样设计说明书，也不是公司内部数据。鞋码轴在源码里按厘米标注；嵌套环图沿用原文件的占位比例。若要改图，先看 出处页 再动 js/index.js。

Not clinical advice, not a sampling-design memo, and not company data. The shoe axis is labeled in centimeters in the source. Inset rings reuse placeholder ratios. To change a chart, read the provenance page before editing js/index.js.
