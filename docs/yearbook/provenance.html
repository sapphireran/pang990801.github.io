仓库不大，但“被首页引用”和“躺在目录里”不是一回事。年鉴把个人源文件分成在线与遗稿两叠，避免以后改错文件。

The repo is small, but “linked from the homepage” and “sitting in the tree” are different. The yearbook splits personal sources into live and leftover stacks so later edits hit the right file.

改图时动哪里

Where to edit a chart

改在线数字只动 js/index.js。改布局动 css/index.css 或它的 Less 源再编译。年鉴数据要同步时重跑 python3 scripts/build-yearbook.py。

Change live numbers only in js/index.js. Change layout in css/index.css or recompile the Less source. Re-run python3 scripts/build-yearbook.py when the yearbook pack should follow.
