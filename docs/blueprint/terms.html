Bilingual terms

同一词，总览和大图里经常错位

原站标题用中文。蓝图工坊把尺码和楦的词做成中英对照，避免「鞋码」被读成零售整数、「胖瘦度」被读成厘米。

尺码与楦

鞋码 / 鞋号
On the dashboard: centimetres of shoe or foot length. In a shop: an integer pair size. The workshop treats the dashboard meaning as length and converts it outward.
Mondopoint
ISO 9407 label equal to foot length in millimetres. 180 means 180 mm, not EU 18.
中国号
Length number in centimetres of the foot. 18.0 号 ≈ Mondopoint 180.
Paris point
2/3 cm. European sizes in this workshop divide last length, not bare foot length, by 6.67 mm.
Barleycorn
1/3 inch ≈ 8.46 mm. UK children’s scale in the converter starts from a 4-inch last.
楦 last
The form the shoe is built on. Last length = foot length + allowance.
放余量
Toe allowance. Default 12 mm here. Not a residual and not measurement error.

几何

脚长
Heel to longest toe, standing. Dashboard scatter y, age bars, and Mondopoint all want this family of length.
跖趾围长
Ball girth through the first and fifth metatarsal heads. Radar axis, 0–100 score.
跗骨围长
Instep / tarsal girth. Radar axis, 0–100 score.
兜跟围长
Heel girth, lowest radar dimension at ages 9–12 in this sample.
胖瘦度
Dashboard index, labelled 脚长/脚宽 in the axis title. Values sit near 23–30 — not a centimetre width.

统计

残差 residual
Observed shoe length minus the sex-specific OLS line. Girl σ ≈ 1.35 cm.
交叉 crossover
Age 11 on the mean curves: boy foot length overtakes girl foot length.
一阶差分 velocity
This year’s mean minus last year’s. Age 9 is negative in the original bars — treat as a subsample kink, not a law of growth.
