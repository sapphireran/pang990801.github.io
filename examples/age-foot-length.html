Age-9 dip and the 10–11 crossover

Both means fall from 8 to 9, then climb. The file is a cross-section, so that dip can be a different group of children rather than a real shrink. Girl minus boy stays positive through age 10 and turns negative at 11. Magnitude stays under 0.5 cm — read the line, not only the bar tops.
